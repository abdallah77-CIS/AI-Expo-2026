Machine Learning

Computers learn patterns from data instead of explicit rules. Place labelled points and watch the classifier learn decision boundaries in real time.

What AI does Uses k-Nearest Neighbours (kNN): for any new point it finds the k closest training examples and votes on the label. Coloured regions show which class the model predicts at every pixel — the boundary shifts live as you add data.

Color & Shape Detector

Machines "see" an image as a grid of coloured pixels. Tap a shape and watch simple pixel math figure out its colour and its form.

What AI does The detector counts pixels by colour to find the dominant colour, then compares the coloured area to its bounding box — this fill ratio is close to 100% for a square, ≈78% for a circle, and ≈50% for a triangle — to guess the shape. No edges, no gradients: just counting and dividing.

Natural Language Processing

AI reads, understands, and responds to human language — finding sentiment, structure, and meaning without understanding emotions.

What AI does Three offline analyses on a built-in dictionary of 30,000+ words, 6,000+ opinion words and thousands of names, cities and companies: Sentiment (strong vs. mild words, negation like "not good", intensifiers like "very", and the clause after "but"), Named entities (people, places, organisations, technologies, dates, money, %), and Part-of-speech tagging (nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs).

Reinforcement Learning

An agent learns to act in an environment by receiving rewards and penalties — no labelled data needed, just trial and error over many episodes.

What AI does The agent (🤖) must reach the goal (🏆) while avoiding hazards (💀) using Q-learning: it stores a table of state→action values, updates them after each move using the Bellman equation (Q(s,a) ← reward + γ·max Q(s',a')), and gradually learns the optimal path through exploration vs exploitation.
